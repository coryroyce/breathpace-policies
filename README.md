## 🔒 Privacy-First Design

Breath Pace is designed with privacy at its core:

- ✅ **Zero data collection** - We don't collect any personal information
- ✅ **100% offline** - Works without internet connection
- ✅ **Local storage only** - All data stays on your device
- ✅ **No analytics** - No tracking or usage monitoring
- ✅ **No third parties** - No external SDKs or data sharing

## 📱 About Breath Pace

Breath Pace is a guided breathing and meditation app for iPhone and iPad that helps you:

- Reduce stress and anxiety
- Improve focus and concentration
- Fall asleep more easily
- Develop a consistent meditation practice

### Features

- Three distinct audio guides (Wave, Breath, Gong)
- Beautiful visual animations (Flower, Waveform)
- Customizable breathing patterns (Box Breathing, 4-7-8, Deep Breathing)
- Immersive haptic feedback
- Flexible session durations (1-30 minutes)
- Dark, calming interface
